● Doctor Clinical Journey
1
1. Provider Login & ABDM Authentication

Doctor signs into Clinical Station via HPR ID (MH-9482). Live OPD Queue loads with ESI triage tags (Yellow, Green, Red).

2
2. Patient Call-In & Video Connect

Doctor calls patient token (#A-042 Ramesh Pawar). Connects live audio/video link to patient mobile or ASHA sub-center.

3
3. Vitals & FHIR Longitudinal History

Reviews real-time vitals (BP 148/92, SpO2 97%, RBS 218 mg/dL) alongside past consultation notes and allergy warnings.

4
4. Diagnosis & Digital e-Prescription

Selects ICD-11 diagnosis, prescribes affordable generic formulary drugs, digitally signs prescription with HPR credentials.

5
5. Referrals & Closed-Loop Sync

eRx sent to Patient App via SMS; follow-up tasks automatically populate in Frontline ASHA worker’s field station.

👩‍⚕️ ASHA & Frontline Health Worker Field Care Flowchart
Community Household Survey • ABHA Generation • NCD Screening • 108 Emergency Dispatch • Follow-Up Loop
● Community Care Continuum
1
1. Daily Schedule & Household Enumeration

ASHA opens mobile station, views assigned village beneficiaries (e.g. Gaikwad Wadi), and conducts home visits.

2
2. Instant ABHA ID & Profile Generation

Verifies existing National Health Cards or creates instant 14-digit ABHA IDs using Aadhaar OTP verification on mobile.

3
3. CBAC NCD Screening & Maternal Vitals

Administers 10-point checklist for Blood Pressure, Diabetes, and High-Risk ANC pregnancy danger signs (Pre-eclampsia, Anemia).

4
4. Teleconsult / 108 Emergency Protocol

Triggers one-touch 108 GPS Ambulance dispatch for critical emergencies or connects patient to Doctor via assisted video call.

5
5. Community Care Loop & Vitals Sync

Delivers generic medicines & IFA tablets; performs bi-weekly home follow-ups and syncs health vitals with the PHC Medical Officer.
